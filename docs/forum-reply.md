Two claims in the original proposal were overstated, and I would rather correct
them than defend them. The swap is atomic: one invoice, one payment hash, both
legs settle or the hub refunds. The leap is not atomic with it, because a cell
cannot carry the RGB++ lock and a channel lock at the same time, so the
conversion is a separate on-chain step that precedes the swap. "Conversion and
swap composed so that they succeed or fail together" is not achievable without
changing the lock model, and I should have said that in the proposal instead of
writing it as a goal. On the refund path, validation failures are covered live
(rate mismatch, expired advertisement, unknown advertisement, all rejected with
no funds moved) and the timeout handling is unit-tested; I did not stage a live
mid-flight timeout.
